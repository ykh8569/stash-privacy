STASH

STASH is a simple link-saving app. One tap to save any link from YouTube, Facebook, Threads, Instagram.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/10/06

STASH does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data to third parties. All links you save are stored locally on your device.

Google Drive Backup

If you enable backup, STASH uses Google Drive API with scopes drive.appdata and drive.file to save a file named linkcatch_backup.json to your own hidden AppData folder (appDataFolder). This file is only accessible by STASH and you. We do not read any other files in your Drive.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data.
